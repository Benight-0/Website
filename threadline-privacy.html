Threadline Local Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Threadline Local is a personal email archive and assistant integration operated by Savyasachi Singh. It connects an authorized Gmail account to the private Threadline archive and Misty assistant. It is intended for the operator's own use; these public policy pages do not make the private archive publicly accessible.

Information accessed

With Google authorization, the integration accesses the account email address, email messages and threads, senders and recipients, subjects, message bodies, timestamps, Gmail labels, drafts, and attachments. It stores OAuth access and refresh tokens to maintain the connection. Google handles the sign-in; the application does not request or store the Google password.

Permissions and purpose

Gmail read access supports importing, searching, displaying and summarizing email. Gmail compose access supports saving requested drafts and sending approved messages. Gmail modify access supports owner-requested mailbox organization, such as label and read-state changes. These permissions are used for the features the owner authorizes, rather than unrestricted automated sending or deletion.

Imported email and derived classifications, summaries, task records and search indexes help the owner find information, identify deadlines, organize correspondence and prepare replies. Sending a personal email requires an exact recipient-and-body preview followed by fresh owner approval through the application's confirmation process.

Storage, processing and service providers

Email content, attachments and derived records are stored in the operator-controlled private archive on a server, with supporting local application data and backups. Access is restricted using account authentication and operating-system permissions. Connections use encrypted transport where applicable, including HTTPS and SSH. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Relevant email content may be sent to the configured AI processing provider, currently OpenAI through its API, to perform the owner's assistant requests, classification or summaries. Requests and results may also appear in the owner's private assistant conversation, currently delivered through Discord. Hosting, authentication and transport providers process data needed to operate their services. Provider-side processing and retention are governed by their service terms and applicable settings. These transfers are limited to operating the authorized features, rather than publishing the mailbox.

Limits on use and sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers, or used by this application to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The application does not provide unrelated people with access to the mailbox. Human access is limited to the owner and specifically authorized access, or access necessary for security, legal obligations, or other uses permitted by Google's policy.

Threadline Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The archive is designed to preserve correspondence. Imported records and derived information remain until the owner requests their deletion; there is no automatic fixed expiry for the archive. Disconnecting the integration or revoking Google authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously imported records. Backup copies and provider-side records may remain under their respective retention arrangements.

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections. For access, correction, deletion, or questions about existing copies and backups, contact the operator below. Deletion requests are handled specifically rather than silently deleting archive data.

Changes and contact

Material changes to data use will be disclosed before applying them, and renewed consent will be requested where required. Contact Savyasachi Singh at emailsavya@gmail.com.
