Threadline Local

A private email archive and personal assistant integration operated by Savyasachi Singh.

What it does

Threadline Local connects an authorized Gmail account to a private archive and the Misty assistant. It lets the owner search and read correspondence, organize messages, summarize information, identify tasks and prepare replies. Requested personal emails are sent only after an exact preview and fresh owner approval.

Gmail connection

The integration requests Gmail read, compose and modify permissions for its email archive, draft, approved-send and owner-requested organization features. Google handles sign-in. Relevant content may be processed using the configured AI service to provide the owner's assistant features, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Personal use

This integration is for the operator's own account. It does not offer public registration. The archive is protected by authentication; this public information page does not expose messages or grant archive access.
